PROBLEMS I SOLVE

What's stopping your store from performing better?

A good-looking store is not enough if Google rejects your products or customers leave without buying. I help find the problem and put the right fix in place.

Merchant Center account suspended

A suspension can stop your products appearing on Google Shopping. I review your website, business information and account setup to identify policy issues and help you correct them.

Products keep getting disapproved

Missing details, incorrect product data and website inconsistencies can affect approval. I check your feed and store information, then help correct the issues.

Visitors don't become customers

Confusing navigation, weak product pages and a difficult checkout can cost sales. I improve the shopping experience so customers can understand your offer and buy with less friction.

Your website is slow or outdated

A poor mobile experience or slow pages can drive visitors away. I build and improve websites that are clear, responsive and easier to navigate.

Shopping Ads aren't performing

Poor setup, weak product data or campaign structure can waste budget. I help organise your Shopping setup around your products and business goals.

FAQ

Questions before getting started?

Here are a few things clients often want to know before starting a project.

How long does a website or store project take?

It depends on the size and requirements. After discussing your needs, I'll provide a realistic timeline before work begins.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the work involved and the condition of your existing setup. I'll review your requirements and send a clear quote before you commit.

Can you work with clients in different time zones?

Yes. I'm based in Nigeria and work remotely with businesses in the UK, EU, US and Canada. We'll agree on communication times and project milestones.

Can you help if my Merchant Center account is suspended?

Yes. I can review your website and account, identify potential policy or data issues and help address them. Google makes the final reinstatement decision, so approval cannot be guaranteed.

What are your payment terms?

Payment terms are agreed before the project starts. Larger projects may be split into agreed milestones. Your quote will explain the schedule and what each stage covers.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. You'll receive the handover and support agreed for your project. Ongoing maintenance or further improvements can be discussed separately.
